# Amplify Systems and Value Engines

This kit is maintained by **Amplify Systems**.

We build practical AI operating systems for teams that want their knowledge, workflows, and customer experience to compound instead of scatter.

The goal is not more tools. The goal is a tighter operating rhythm: clearer promises, cleaner systems, better customer journeys, and fewer fragile manual handoffs.

## What Is a Value Engine?

A **Value Engine** is the system that turns attention, trust, and delivery into real customer value.

In simple terms:

- Your **Growth Engine** earns attention and trust from the right people.
- Your **Fulfillment Engine** delivers the promise after they buy.
- Your **Value Engine** is both of those working together around a clear promise.

Front matter and documentation-as-code are part of that operating system. When the work is structured, agents can find the right context, humans can navigate the system, and teams can move faster without losing the plot.
